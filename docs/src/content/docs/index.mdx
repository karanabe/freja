  <section class="landing-intro" aria-labelledby="landing-intro-title">
    <p class="landing-eyebrow">Interactive by default</p>
    <h2 id="landing-intro-title">Inspect local traffic without hiding the decision path.</h2>
    <p>Start locally with the TUI, active enforcement, and bounded interactive HTTP decisions. Headless operation disables hooks but keeps enforcement active. TLS interception, remote exposure, and payload capture remain explicit opt-ins.</p>
  </section>

  <div class="landing-principles" aria-label="Freja principles">
    <section>
      <span aria-hidden="true">01</span>
      <h3>Explain every action</h3>
      <p>Every policy decision carries its generation, matched rule, observed facts, and final action.</p>
    </section>
    <section>
      <span aria-hidden="true">02</span>
      <h3>Bound every resource</h3>
      <p>Connections, body prefixes, channels, timeouts, paused flows, and certificate caches have explicit limits.</p>
    </section>
    <section>
      <span aria-hidden="true">03</span>
      <h3>Keep evidence local</h3>
      <p>Audit records are redacted, hash chained, optionally signed, and replayable against a new policy.</p>
    </section>
  </div>
