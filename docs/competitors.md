# Competitive Positioning

VEY, Rama, mitmproxy, Hudsucker, and Squid already exist and provide important
overlapping capabilities. Freja does not claim that Rust, ACL support, forward
proxying, TLS interception, L4/L7 support, or performance alone are unique
differentiators.

Freja focuses on explainable deterministic policy decisions, typed hooks, a
shared live/headless/replay pipeline, privacy-aware tamper-evident audit
records, and local-first operation in controlled or air-gapped environments.

The maintained comparison and project-specific caveats are
[`src/content/docs/developer/competitive-positioning.md`](src/content/docs/developer/competitive-positioning.md).
